# Give the community host authority over discussions

An account's home server owns its identity, while a community's host owns authoritative discussion and membership state. Remote authors submit authenticated actions to that host; they may request edits or deletion, and moderators may remove content but not rewrite it under the author's name. This concentrates community authorization and moderation decisions in one authority rather than coordinating competing authoritative copies, at the cost of requiring host acceptance before confirming publication.
